How it all started

One idea.
A whole lot of spark.

Our story started with three college students asking a simple question: “What if planning a great night out was as easy as picking a baseball game?” Between classes, group projects, and busy schedules, we wanted a place where students, families, and local fans could come together and feel part of Columbia.

That conversation became our fictional Columbia Fireflies business concept. By combining baseball, themed events, and group experiences, we imagined a business that sells more than tickets—it helps people make memories.

Our goal: turn an ordinary evening into a night worth cheering about.

Fan FAQ.

Click a question to reveal the answer.

Do I need to know baseball to have fun?

Not at all! Our concept welcomes first-time fans as much as baseball experts. Come for the atmosphere, enjoy the food, and cheer when your friends cheer.

Can our student group plan an outing?

Absolutely—that’s what our fictional group experiences are designed for. Imagine bringing your classmates, campus club, or study group together for a night away from the textbooks.

Why the name “Fireflies”?

For our student concept, fireflies represent the little sparks that make a night memorable: bright lights, big cheers, and time spent together.

What if my team loses?

The scoreboard might disagree with you, but a night with your favorite people still counts as a win. Snacks help, too.

Can I buy real tickets on this website?

No. This website is a student project and does not sell tickets, accept payments, or represent the official Columbia Fireflies organization.
